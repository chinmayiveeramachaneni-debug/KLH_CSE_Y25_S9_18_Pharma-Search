Paracetamol is a commonly used medicine for reducing fever and relieving mild to moderate pain. It is available in different tablet and syrup forms.

Paracetamol should be taken according to the recommended dosage provided by a qualified healthcare professional or the medicine label.

Ibuprofen is a non-steroidal anti-inflammatory medicine commonly used to reduce pain, inflammation, and fever.

Ibuprofen may be used for conditions such as headache, muscle pain, toothache, and menstrual pain. It should be used according to medical advice.

Amoxicillin is an antibiotic used to treat certain bacterial infections. It belongs to the penicillin group of antibiotics.

Amoxicillin may be prescribed for bacterial infections affecting the respiratory tract, ears, skin, and other parts of the body.

Azithromycin is an antibiotic used for certain bacterial infections. It may be prescribed by a healthcare professional when appropriate.

Cetirizine is an antihistamine commonly used to relieve allergy symptoms such as sneezing, runny nose, and itching.

Omeprazole is a medicine that reduces the production of stomach acid. It is commonly used for conditions related to excess stomach acid.

Metformin is commonly prescribed to help control blood glucose levels in people with type 2 diabetes. It is generally used along with diet and exercise as advised by a healthcare professional.

Amlodipine is a medicine commonly used to help control high blood pressure. It may also be prescribed for certain heart-related conditions.

Antibiotics are medicines used to treat bacterial infections. They should be taken only when prescribed or recommended by a qualified healthcare professional.

Medicine dosage depends on several factors including the medicine, age, medical condition, and other medicines being taken.

Some medicines may cause side effects. Common side effects can include nausea, headache, dizziness, stomach discomfort, or drowsiness depending on the medicine.

Patients should read the medicine label carefully and follow the instructions provided by a healthcare professional.

Medicines should be stored according to the instructions on their packaging and should be kept away from children.

Patients should inform their healthcare professional about allergies and other medicines they are taking before starting a new medicine.

Expired medicines should not be used. Check the expiry date printed on the medicine package before use.

Pharma Search uses the KMP string matching algorithm to search pharmaceutical information efficiently.

The search module accepts a medicine name or keyword and identifies matching occurrences in the pharmaceutical corpus.

The KMP algorithm uses a Longest Prefix Suffix array, also called the LPS array, to avoid unnecessary comparisons during string matching.
